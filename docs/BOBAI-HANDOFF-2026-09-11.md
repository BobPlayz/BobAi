Controls are `D:\BobAi\bob-training.cmd status`, `pause`, `resume`, and `stop`. Status persists stage, profile, step/total, percentage, loss, validation loss when available, elapsed time, ETA, and checkpoint path. Resume restores the saved pipeline stage/profile/settings from `training-pipeline.json` automatically.

USB-specific checkpointing was hardened. Normal training no longer writes a full checkpoint every optimizer step. Regular checkpoint writes are spaced out to reduce large repeated writes to flash storage. Pause and stop are cooperative: the trainer notices the control request after the current optimizer step, writes `latest.pt` immediately, records paused/stopped status, and exits cleanly. This frees the laptop resources for interactive BobAI use without relying on force-killing the trainer. `latest.pt` is the resumable checkpoint; `best.pt` is the best validation checkpoint when available.
